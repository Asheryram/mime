The short version

Outbound finds companies that match what you're looking for, finds who to email there, and sends each one a short email from your own Gmail with your CV and a cover letter written for that company. If they don't reply, it follows up once. It runs on its own; you only step in when it emails you that something needs you, or when a company writes back.

What happens, in order

  1. Searchevery 6 hours
    Each line on your Settings page is a search. New companies that match are added to your list; ones you've already got are skipped.
  2. Find an emailsame run
    It looks on each company's website for a careers or contact address, and rejects ones that look wrong (personal, no-reply, someone else's domain).
  3. Pick a rolesame run
    It reads the company's site and picks which of your Roles fits best, so a frontend shop gets your frontend pitch and CV.
  4. Ask you when it isn't sureYOU
    If the company name looks odd or no role clearly fits, that company waits on Needs you instead of being sent on a guess. Companies with no public email (about half of small ones) are kept under Companies → Skipped: add an email there if you find one, or reach them on LinkedIn, and you get one email listing them, each with a Fix button. Each takes under a minute.
  5. Sendweekdays at {{ "%02d:%02d"|format(send_hour, send_minute) }} GMT
    Up to {{ daily_limit }} a day, from your Gmail, each with the right CV and a cover letter addressed to that company. The rest wait for the next weekday; see them on Up next.
  6. Follow up onceafter {{ followup_days }} days
    No reply after {{ followup_days }} days? One short, polite follow-up goes out in the same thread. Never more than one.
  7. Notice repliesevery 6 hours
    It checks your inbox (read-only) for replies and bounces, and stops following up with anyone who answered.
  8. You replyYOU
    When a company writes back, answer them yourself in Gmail like any email. Then open the company on Awaiting reply and record what they said (see below), so the system knows what to do next.

When a company writes back

Open the company and choose what they said:

  • Interview / next steps: nothing automatic is sent; it's yours from here.
  • Not hiring now: check back later: pick when (e.g. 3 months). On that date it emails you a drafted check-in to read and send.
  • Not interested: it's closed and nothing more is sent to them.
  • They referred me to someone else: enter the new address and choose to send your CV, cover letter or both. You'll see the email before it goes.

Don't want a company emailed at all? Open it and use Don't email this one. It stays out for good, even if a search finds it again.

Writing good searches

One search per line on Settings: what to look for | how many (1–25) | which role.

fintech startups in Accra with in-house engineering teams | 10 | frontend
remote-first SaaS companies hiring platform engineers in Europe | 15 | devops
healthtech companies in Nairobi building patient apps | 8 | fullstack

Describe the companies, not the job title: industry, place, what they build. Specific beats broad: "logistics startups in Lagos" finds better fits than "tech companies". The role is only a starting guess; it still checks each company's site. Start with 3–5 searches and adjust after a week, based on who replies.

If something's not working

Nothing has been sent yet

{% if is_member %}Check the Get set up list on Overview: nothing sends until every step is ticked. {% endif %}Then check Up next: if companies are there, they go out on the next weekday at {{ "%02d:%02d"|format(send_hour, send_minute) }} GMT. If it's empty, look at Needs you, or make your searches more specific.

It says Gmail rejected my password

Make a new app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords (signed in to the Gmail you send from), paste it on {% if is_member %}Profile{% else %}your secrets{% endif %}, and use Test Gmail login. Changing your Google password cancels old app passwords.

Searches find nothing, or emails aren't being found

Usually your free credit has run out. Check Exa billing (searching) and Apify billing (finding emails; the free $5 resets monthly). Otherwise, make searches more specific.

It sent a company the wrong role

Add clearer keywords to each role on Roles, so companies match the right one. For a company not yet sent, open it and change its role.

I want to stop for a while

Turn your roles off on Roles. Nothing sends until you turn one back on; everything else is kept.

Can anyone else see my companies or keys?

No. {% if is_member %}Your workspace is yours alone: other users can't see it. Your keys are stored encrypted and never shown again, not even to you.{% else %}Each invited user has a separate workspace.{% endif %}
